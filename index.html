Kinetic Coach

Rehabilitation that adapts to the patient.

Kinetic Coach reads how a patient actually moves — correcting form in real time and adjusting difficulty rep by rep, instead of running everyone through the same fixed programme.

Watch the pitch See how it works
The pitch

Two minutes on what we're building

The problem

Recovery is personal. Most rehab tools treat it like a checklist.

01

No form feedback at home

Patients get printed sheets or videos with no way to check whether they're doing an exercise correctly.

02

Gaps between visits

Therapists can't watch every patient continuously, so form errors and setbacks go uncaught until the next appointment.

03

Fixed difficulty, changing bodies

Static resistance and rep counts don't track how recovery changes week to week — causing plateaus or re-injury.

How it works

One loop, running every session

Sense what the patient is doing, interpret it against their own baseline, and respond — automatically, before the next rep.

Step 1

Sense

Camera-based pose tracking plus wearable sensors capture joint angles, range and tempo during each exercise.

Step 2

Understand

A movement-quality model compares form against the patient's own baseline and recovery stage — not a generic ideal.

Step 3

Respond

Real-time correction cues, plus adaptive resistance from the connected device, adjust difficulty as the patient improves.

The product

Two views, one system

Patient app

Live form feedback

On-screen cues during each exercise, a session score, and a clear recommendation for what to adjust next time — so patients aren't guessing between visits.

Therapist dashboard

Adherence and progress at a glance

Trends across sessions, flagged form issues, and adaptive-resistance history — so check-ins start from what actually changed.

Why it's different

Nobody closes the loop

Existing tools either count generic reps or provide fixed resistance with no intelligence layer. Kinetic Coach connects sensing to response.
